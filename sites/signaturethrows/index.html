Questions

Can I use my krewe's logo?

Yes, if you're a member ordering for your krewe or float, or you otherwise have permission to use it. We can't print sports team logos, cartoon characters, or other brands you don't own.

What if I don't have artwork?

Describe what you want in the notes, such as your krewe name, colors, and a symbol, and we'll design a simple throw for your proof.

What are they made of?

A sturdy, lightweight plastic called PLA, printed in layered colors. They're made to be thrown, but please aim for hands, not heads.

Can I order fewer than 25?

Single pieces aren't available yet. Email us if you need a small number of samples before a bigger order.

Where do I pick up?

In Metairie, by appointment. We'll email you the address and a time once your order is ready.
