Shipping and pickup

Last updated November 10, 2026

Here's how long orders take and how you get them.

Production time

We email your proof within two business days of your order. Most printed throws are ready 7 to 10 business days after you approve it. Large orders and orders during the last weeks before Mardi Gras can take longer, and we'll tell you the expected date on your proof.

Pickup

Pickup is free, in Metairie, by appointment. When your order is ready we'll email you the address and available times.

Shipping

We ship anywhere in the United States for a flat rate shown at checkout. Orders usually arrive 2 to 5 business days after they ship, and we email tracking when they leave.

Cups and plain beads

Custom cups and plain bead cases ship directly from our suppliers to the address you give us, usually within 5 to 10 business days. Allow extra time in January and February.

Order deadlines

For Carnival 2027, order printed throws and custom cups by January 15 and plain beads by January 20. We'll still try to fill later orders, but we can't promise they'll arrive before your parade.

Lost or damaged packages

If a package is lost or arrives damaged, email us with your order number and photos. We'll reprint or refund the affected items.

Questions

Email hello@signaturethrows.com. We reply within one business day.
