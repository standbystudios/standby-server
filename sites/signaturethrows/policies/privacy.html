Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026

We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address, and phone number, if you give it
  • Your shipping address, if you choose shipping
  • The artwork and notes you send us
  • Your order history with us

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details directly; we never see or store your full card number. Stripe's privacy policy covers how it handles payment information.

How we use it

We use your information to make your proof, fill your order, contact you about it, and keep business and tax records. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us fill your order: Stripe for payments, our shipping carriers, and, for cups and beads, the supplier that ships them to you.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. We delete your artwork 12 months after your order unless you ask us to keep it for a reorder.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you, except for records we're required to keep.

Questions

Email hello@signaturethrows.com. We reply within one business day.
