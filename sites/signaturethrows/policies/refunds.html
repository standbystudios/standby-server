Refunds and cancellations

Last updated November 10, 2026

Every throw is made to order with your design, so our policy depends on how far your order has gone.

Before you approve your proof

Cancel any time before you approve your proof and we'll refund your payment in full.

After you approve your proof

Once you approve a proof, we start printing and can't take the order back or refund it, because the pieces can't be sold to anyone else.

If something is wrong with your order

Tell us within 7 days of pickup or delivery and send a photo. If a piece is broken, misprinted, or doesn't match the proof you approved, we'll reprint it at no cost. If there isn't time to reprint before your parade, we'll refund those pieces instead.

Cups and plain beads

Custom printed cups are made by our cup supplier to your approved design and can't be cancelled once they go to print. Plain bead cases can be cancelled until they ship. If cups or beads arrive damaged, send us a photo within 7 days and we'll replace or refund the damaged items.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank usually shows them within 5 to 10 business days.

Questions

Email hello@signaturethrows.com. We reply within one business day.
